I'm a PhD candidate in Astrophysics at Federal University of Sergipe (UFS). I have experience with exoplanets and Galactic Archaeology. 

During my master's, I worked on the construction of the largest orbital parameter catalog, accepted for publication in ApJS (you can check the ArXiv manuscript here https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23411). 

Now, I'm trying to link both areas and place the exoplanetary population in the context of the Milky Way's history. 

For those in Brazil, here is my Lattes CV: http://lattes.cnpq.br/8888652953547154.

Life beyond work
======
Apart from academia, I enjoy reading, going to the cinema, spending time with loved ones, and, as someone from Minas Gerais, I love going to the beach. 
I truly believe that education is the best way to transform people’s lives and the world itself.
